# Reflection – Mission 3: Become a Multi-Cloud Explorer

## 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?

Among the three cloud platforms, **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** impressed me the most because of its strong capabilities in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes. I found it interesting how cloud platforms provide powerful technologies that organizations can use without having to build all of their infrastructure themselves.

## 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?

AWS, Azure, and GCP provide many similar cloud services, including **computing, storage, networking, databases, and identity management**. They also provide scalable infrastructure and management tools that allow organizations to deploy and manage applications in the cloud. Although their service names and features differ, they solve many of the same business and technical problems.

## 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?

Business requirements are important because there is no single cloud provider that is best for every organization. Factors such as **budget, existing technology, performance, security, scalability, and workload requirements** should be considered before choosing a provider. For example, an organization that uses many Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure, while an organization focused on AI and Kubernetes may prefer GCP.

## 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that selecting a cloud provider is not simply about choosing the most popular platform. I learned to examine a client's requirements first and then match those requirements with the strengths and services of each cloud provider. This helped me understand how technical decisions can support real business goals.

## 5. How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved after completing this mission?

My **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** became more organized and professional after completing this mission. I added research files, comparison tables, client recommendations, screenshots, and a reflection. This activity also improved my understanding of AWS, Azure, and GCP while helping me practice technical documentation using Markdown. Overall, the mission improved both my cloud computing knowledge and my ability to analyze and recommend cloud solutions.
